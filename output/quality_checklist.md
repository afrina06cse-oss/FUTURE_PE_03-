# SEO Quality Checklist

## Quality Assurance Checklist

* [x] The article matches the intended reader question and local search intent.
* [x] One clear H1 is used with logical H2 and H3 headings.
* [x] Keywords are used naturally without unnecessary repetition.
* [x] The content provides practical information for readers.
* [x] Internal links are planned using relevant anchor text.
* [x] Local business details are not presented as verified facts without checking.
* [x] No fake ratings, reviews, addresses, prices, opening hours, or first-hand claims are included.
* [x] SEO title and content structure are reviewed.
* [x] Readability and accessibility are considered.
* [x] The content requires human review before publication.

## Publish-Readiness Review

The content is designed as a demonstration project. Before publishing, local business information should be verified using current and reliable sources.

Important details such as addresses, opening hours, prices, menus, ratings, reviews, and availability should not be assumed or copied from outdated sources.

## Limitations

This project is based on a hypothetical local café content scenario. It does not use live keyword-volume data, verified café websites, or real customer research.

The generated content should therefore be treated as a content demonstration rather than verified local business information.

The project does not claim that the content will rank at a specific position in search results.

## Next Improvements

Future improvements could include:

1. Validate keywords using an appropriate keyword research tool.
2. Review current search results and local competitors.
3. Replace general guidance with verified business information.
4. Test the prompts using a real business brief.
5. Compare multiple generated outlines and content drafts.
6. Record human edits and improve the prompt system based on the review.
7. Verify all local details before publication.
